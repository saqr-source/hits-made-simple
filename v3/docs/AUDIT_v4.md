# HITS Made Simple v4.0: Independent QA Audit

Date: 2026-10-21 · Build: `v3/assets/data.js` v4.0 (generated by `tools/build_v4.py`) · Site: http://localhost:8765/v3/
Method: I ran a Playwright/Chromium walkthrough of every stop in both languages, both roles, at 360×740 and 390×844. For each step it captured screenshots, measured scroll and the Back/Next positions, and read which screen file was showing. It also answered every quiz, scored it and replayed it. Separately, every step's `hot` and `type` box from data.js was drawn on its screen (`ov/*.jpg`), all 84 screens were checked by eye, and unused raw screenshots were reviewed to see whether missing stops could be covered.
All evidence paths are relative to `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/19d6f8f5-c286-53d2-bab7-4ae3f11ea842/scratchpad/audit4/`.

## What passed cleanly
- Screen language: 100% of rendered steps match the interface language.
- Arabic copy: no English words anywhere except HITS, OFFICE 365 and numbers (data scan plus a scan of the rendered text).
- "Get started" is first for every role and language combination.
- The Home button is on every non-home view.
- No vertical scrolling in any step and no sideways scrolling anywhere, at both sizes. Back/Next placement is identical in both languages.
- Typed "12345" and the password dots sit inside the real fields in both languages. No real credentials are shown.
- Scoring is 10 per stop plus 10 for a right first answer. Replays earn nothing. A wrong answer shows the right answer, its reason, and an encouraging line.
- No JavaScript errors across 4 full runs.
